6. Daisy Blanket 🌼

🔒 PRO Pattern - Unlock to view!
1. Yellow yarn - Magic Ring
2. R1: 6 SC in MR (6) Pull tight SL ST join
3. Cut yellow, attach white in any SC of yellow
4. R2: [CH2, 1 DC, CH2, SL ST in next SC] = 1 petal
5. Repeat [CH2, 1 DC, CH2, SL ST next] x12 = 12 petals
6. 12 white petals around yellow Daisy ready
7. SL ST first petal base FO 5cm tail
8. Weave tails back needle Hide ends
9. One daisy 3cm cute small
10. Make 35 such daisy flowers Keep ready
1. Pastel color attach any space between petals
2. R1: [three double crochets in one stitch CH2] x4 = 4 corners SL ST
3. Square around circle daisy 4 CH2 corners
4. R2: SC all around Corners three double crochets in one stitch CH2 three double crochets in one stitch Sides three double crochets in one stitch
5. Bigger 12 per side SL ST join
6. R3: SC all around Corners three double crochets in one stitch CH2 three double crochets in one stitch Sides 1 DC each
7. Corners same three double crochets in one stitch CH2 three double crochets in one stitch
8. R4: SC all around Corners three double crochets in one stitch CH2 three double crochets in one stitch 10x10cm
9. FO One square ready Flower + pastel square
10. Make 35 squares total Same or mix colors
1. Lay 35 floor Arrange 5 ROWS x 7 COLS = 35 Squares
2. Join method SC Join white seam visible OR Whip invisible Recommend SC white
3. Join 7 squares = 1 row: 2 squares right sides together white SC both loops 1 SC each CH1
4. Join 3rd same till 7 = 1 long row Make 5 such rows Each 7 squares
5. Join 5 rows: 2 rows together white SC both edges 1 SC each whole row length
6. Repeat Join all 5 rows big blanket 150x110cm 35 attached
7. Check no holes whip back loops zig-zag invisible if whip
8. BORDER: Attach white any corner big blanket R1 SC all around corners 3 SC in one
9. R2: SC all around Corners 3 SC in one Border thicker
10. R3: SC all around FO Done! 35 joined cozy blanket white border! 🌼

About Me 💖

Hiii! I'm Diya Sahai from Gurugram, owner of Tiny Twirl Cloud! ☁️
I started crocheting 1 year ago from YouTube with my first granny square!
I love pastel colors like pink, lavender and mint - tiny yarn magic is my fav! ✨
This site has my 6 favourite patterns - 3 PRO + 3 FREE with full love! 💖

PRO patterns: Bunny, Bow + Strawberry scrunchies with thick black band, and Daisy blanket with 35 squares!
FREE patterns: Granny Square with three double crochets in one stitch and Flower Headband with CH according to head size!
Headband tip: Flowers cover whole stitched part - no plain band visible, only flowers! 🌸

Scrunchies trick: Use thick black hair band, SC 30 around it, then ruffle with R2 2DC each and R3 [DC INC]!
Bow trick: Attach bow on SL ST seam outside to hide seam, sew middle 4-5 times and knot inside! 🎀
