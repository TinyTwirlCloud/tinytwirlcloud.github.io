About Me - Diya Sahai 💖

Para 1 - My Story: Hii! I'm Diya Sahai, founder of Tiny Twirl Cloud! Started at 12, watching tutorials late night with small hook and leftover yarn. What started as tiny hobby to make granny square turned into my biggest passion. Every stitch has my heart, love, patience. My first bunny was wonky but I loved it! Handmade is about love, not perfection! 🧶💖

Para 2 - About Patterns: At Tiny Twirl Cloud, 26 patterns! 6 FULL with brief assembly - Bunny, Bow Scrunchie, Strawberry, Granny, Headband, Daisy. Every pattern brief + different lines - like HEAD, BODY, EARS, HANDS, LEGS, ASSEMBLY - easy to follow! Bunny: head separately, body separately, ears floppy, arms legs small, then assemble tight 360 deg. Same for all - band, bow, leaves, seeds, all separate lines! 20 coming soon with cherry coaster! ☁️✨

Para 3 - Thank You: This is my tiny dream true! Never thought hobby would become where you send requests and want to learn! Thank you for being here, supporting small business, loving bunnies! Every like, comment, fav means world! Still learning, promise more twirls, patterns, love! This is my cloud, my happy place, yours too! Keep twirling with me! - Diya 💖🧶☁️
