5. Flower Headband 🌸 - SECOND LAST WALA - FREE ✨ - FULL!

⭐ THIS IS SECOND LAST WALA - HEADBAND!
Step 1 - CH according to your head size:
• Measure head with tape OR use yarn around head
• Adult: 70 CH (56cm head)
• Kid: 60 CH (50cm head)
• Baby: 50 CH (40cm head)
• CH according to your head size - This is base chain!
Step 2 - Stitched part:
R1: CH according to your head size (70/60/50) as per above
R2: SC in 2nd CH from hook, SC all around - Join with SL ST to first SC to make circle - (70 SC)
R3: SC all around (70)
R4: SC all around (70)
R5: SC all around (70)
R6: SC all around (70) - FO
You now have a stitched band circle - Approx 5cm wide, stretchy!
Step 3 - Flowers to cover whole stitched part (No plain band visible!):
Flower Pattern: Magic Ring [CH2, 2 DC, CH2, SL ST] repeat 5 times = 5 petals flower - Make 1 flower
Make 8-12 such flowers (mix colors pink/white/yellow)
How to attach flowers: Sew flowers side by side on whole stitched part of headband - Cover full band - Stitch flowers touching each other - No plain band should be visible - Only flowers visible!
Use needle, sew each flower base 2-3 times on band - Flowers cover whole stitched part completely!
Final: Headband ready! Flowers cover whole stitched part - No plain band visible - Super cute! 🌸✨

6. Daisy Blanket 🌼 - 35 Squares - PRO - 3 Paragraphs!

🔒 PRO
Para 1 - Daisy Flower (10 lines)
1 Yellow MR 2 R1 6SC 3 Attach white 4 R2 [CH2 1DC CH2 SL ST] x1 petal 5 Repeat x12 =12 petals 6 Daisy shape 7 SL ST FO 5cm 8 Weave tails 9 One flower 3cm 10 Make 35
Para 2 - Square (10 lines)
1 Pastel attach between petals 2 R1 [three double crochets in one stitch CH2] x4 4 corners 3 Square around daisy 4 R2 SC all around Corners three double crochets in one stitch CH2 three double crochets in one stitch Sides three double crochets in one stitch 5 Bigger 12 per side 6 R3 SC Corners three double crochets in one stitch CH2 three double crochets in one stitch Sides 1DC each 7 Corners same 8 R4 SC Corners three double crochets in one stitch CH2 three double crochets in one stitch 10x10cm 9 FO one square ready 10 Make 35
Para 3 - Join 35 (10 lines)
1 Lay 35 floor 5 ROWS x7 COLS=35 2 SC Join white seam pretty OR whip invisible 3 Join 7 squares=1 row 2 squares right sides together white SC through both loops 1SC each across CH1 4 Join 3rd same Repeat 7=1 row Make 5 rows 5 Join 5 rows together 2 rows right sides together SC through both edges 1SC each 6 Repeat Join 5 rows big blanket 150x110cm 35 attached 7 Check no holes whip back loops zig-zag invisible 8 BORDER white any corner R1 SC all around big blanket corners 3SC in one 9 R2 SC all around corners 3SC 10 R3 SC FO Done! 🌼
